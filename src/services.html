Digital Services

From browsing our catalogue online to booking a quiet study room, Readers' Haven makes the library easier to use, both inside the building and from home.

📖 Online Catalogue

Search every book in the library and check if it is available before you visit.

  1. Open the catalogue from any library computer or your phone.
  2. Search by title, author or subject.
  3. Place a hold and collect the book within 3 days.

Available: 24/7

📱 E-Books & Audiobooks

Borrow digital books and listen to audiobooks on your phone, tablet or laptop.

  1. Log in with your membership number.
  2. Borrow up to 3 e-books at a time.
  3. Titles return automatically after 14 days.

Available: 24/7

🪑 Study Room Booking

Reserve a quiet room for group study or individual work.

  1. Book at the front desk or by phone.
  2. Each booking is for up to 2 hours.
  3. Rooms hold 2 to 6 people.

Timings: Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM

🖨️ Printing & Scanning

Print, photocopy and scan documents at the self-service station.

  • Black & white: PKR 10 per page
  • Colour: PKR 30 per page
  • Scanning to email: Free

Timings: Mon–Sat, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

📶 Free Wi-Fi

Fast, free internet for all members across the library.

  1. Connect to the "ReadersHaven-Guest" network.
  2. Enter your membership number to log in.
  3. Each session lasts 3 hours.

Available: During library opening hours
